## All Available Quick Answers

### **1. Term Insurance Questions**

#### Q: "What is term insurance?"
**A:** Term life insurance provides pure death protection for a fixed period (10-40 years). Cheapest life insurance option (₹500-1500/month for ₹1cr). Best for families with dependents.

#### Q: "Is term insurance worth it?" 
**A:** Absolutely! For ₹500/month you get ₹1cr coverage. That's almost unlimited ROI if needed. Essential for any earning member with dependents.

---

### **2. Health Insurance Questions**

#### Q: "What is health insurance?"
**A:** Health insurance covers medical expenses during hospitalization, treatment, and diagnostics. Protects your savings from medical emergencies. Recommend ₹5-10L sum insured.

#### Q: "Should I buy health insurance?"
**A:** Absolutely essential! Medical emergencies can cost ₹2-5 lakhs easily. Health insurance at ₹200-300/month saves you from bankruptcy.

---

### **3. Critical Illness Questions**

#### Q: "What is critical illness insurance?"
**A:** Lump sum payment upon diagnosis of serious illnesses (cancer, heart attack, stroke). Supplements health insurance. Covers lost income during recovery. ₹25-50L coverage recommended.

#### Q: "Should I buy critical illness insurance?"
**A:** If you're main earner: YES. Lost income during illness is bigger risk than treatment cost. Cheapest rider option: ₹40-80/month for ₹25L.

---

### **4. Coverage Planning Questions**

#### Q: "How much insurance do I need?"
**A:** Term insurance: 10-15x annual income + debts. Health: ₹5-10L sum insured. Critical illness: ₹25-50L. Pension: 25x annual expenses for 30 years retirement.

#### Q: "How much life insurance coverage should I buy?"
**A:** Use formula: 10-15x annual income + outstanding loans. Example: ₹10L annual income → ₹1-1.5cr term insurance needed. Plus ₹50L for loans, ₹50L for kids' education.

---

### **5. Cost & Premium Questions**

#### Q: "What is the premium for term insurance?"
**A:** Depends on age, health, term length, sum insured. Young (25-30): ₹500-1000/month for ₹1cr, 20-year term. Find exact rate using age/health/coverage on our calculator.

#### Q: "Is term insurance expensive?"
**A:** No! ₹500-1000/month gets you ₹1cr term + ₹5L health insurance. That's almost unlimited ROI if needed. Less than a coffee per day!

---

### **6. NPS & Pension Questions**

#### Q: "What is NPS?"
**A:** National Pension System. Government-backed retirement plan. Market-linked returns (6-8%). Lower charges. Up to ₹2L tax deduction. Withdraw 60% at retirement, convert 40% to pension.

#### Q: "Which insurance plan is best?"
**A:** No single best plan. Ideal combination: Term insurance (₹500/month) + Health insurance (₹200/month) + Critical illness rider (₹100/month) + NPS (₹2000/month) = ₹2800/month for complete protection.

---

### **7. Insurance Comparison Questions**

#### Q: "What is the difference between term and whole life insurance?"
**A:** Term life: ₹500-1500/month for ₹1cr, 10-40 year coverage. Whole life: ₹5000-15000/month for ₹1cr, lifetime coverage. Most people need term. Whole life for legacy building only if affordable.

#### Q: "Should I buy health or critical illness insurance?"
**A:** Both! Health covers actual treatment costs. Critical illness pays lump sum for specific illnesses. Ideal: Health insurance (mandatory) + Critical illness rider (supplementary).

#### Q: "Should I take ULIP or endowment?"
**A:** ULIP for young people (15+ years), market returns possible (8-12%). Endowment for conservative investors, guaranteed returns (4-5%). ULIP usually better if you can wait 20+ years.

---

### **8. Claims Questions**

#### Q: "How do I file an insurance claim?"
**A:** Health insurance: Notify before admission → Cashless treatment at network hospital. Life insurance: Submit death certificate + policy → Claim settled in 15-30 days. Keep all bills/documents organized.

#### Q: "How long does a health insurance claim take?"
**A:** Cashless at network hospital: Instant (paid directly by insurer). Reimbursement: 7-10 days for paperless, 15-20 days for paper claims. Faster if documents are complete.

#### Q: "What documents do I need for insurance claim?"
**A:** Health: Hospital bill, discharge summary, medical reports, prescriptions, ID proof, payment receipt. Life: Death certificate, policy document, nominee ID proof, last premium receipt.

---

### **9. Policy-Specific Questions**

#### Q: "Should I buy family floater or individual health insurance?"
**A:** Family floater costs less (₹200-300/month for ₹5L for family of 4). Individual more flexible (₹100-150/month per person). Choose floater for large families, individual if 3+ earners.

#### Q: "What is the claim settlement process timeline?"
**A:** Timeline: 2-3 days acknowledgment → 7-15 days processing → 2-5 days approval → 1-3 days payment. Total: Usually 30-45 days end-to-end from claim filing.

#### Q: "What happens if health insurance bill exceeds sum insured?"
**A:** You pay the excess. Insurer covers up to sum insured. Hence increase sum insured planning. Better to over-insure than under-insure.

---

### **10. General Insurance Questions**

#### Q: "Why is insurance premium increasing with age?"
**A:** Medical claims increase with age due to health risks. Insurance pools risk - younger people provide cheaper rates. Lock in rates now while young!

#### Q: "Should I buy multiple health insurance policies?"
**A:** Yes! Multiple health policies don't duplicate benefits. All claims are valid from all insurers. Recommended: Primary policy ₹5L + Supplementary policy ₹5L.

#### Q: "Any way to reduce insurance premiums?"
**A:** Healthy lifestyle (exercise, no smoking), annual health checkups, bundle policies, buy online, pay annually (5-8% discount). Group discounts 10-15% if 4+ family members.

---

### **11. NPS vs Traditional Comparison**

#### Q: "Should I invest in NPS or traditional endowment?"
**A:** NPS better for most: 6-8% returns, lower charges (0.3%), ₹2L tax deduction. Traditional: 4-5% guaranteed, higher charges. NPS superior unless you want guaranteed returns only.

#### Q: "What's the monthly pension from NPS?"
**A:** Example: ₹50L corpus (saved over 30 years) → ~₹20-25K monthly pension at 60 years. Depends on retirement age and investment returns.

---

### **12. Coverage Adequacy Questions**

#### Q: "Do I need health insurance if employer provides?"
**A:** Yes! Employer coverage ends if you change jobs. Personal policy provides continuity and additional safety. Buy personal policy for permanent protection.

#### Q: "Can I increase health insurance coverage later?"
**A:** Yes! Most plans allow increasing coverage. Some may require medical tests for large increases. Lock in maximum coverage while young and healthy.

#### Q: "What room rent limit should I choose in health insurance?"
**A:** Avoid capped room rent. Choose unlimited or 5% of sum insured. Room rent at good hospitals is ₹5000-15000/day. Capped room rent can trigger deductions.
